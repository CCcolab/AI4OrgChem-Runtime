## Fixed points and derivatives

SCF fixed-point convergence, energy stationarity on the allowed tangent space and second-order stability are different questions. Idempotency, energy closure, a small commutator or exit zero cannot replace them. A difference between a masked operator and an unmasked derivative is not by itself a method error: forbidden variations must be excluded before comparing derivatives.
