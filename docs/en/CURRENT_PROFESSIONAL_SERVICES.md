## Conditional-state research boundary

V2 is implemented and has been diagnosed, but no qualifying user conditional endpoint was obtained. A saved sample has a small commutator residual, gap about 10⁻¹⁵ Eh and mapping residual about 0.43. A later native-root attempt completed 139 NR evaluations and 138 successful callbacks before one deadline failure; no normal root return or completed tails/closure. Counts have different meanings. A preparation without a grid reports grid integration as NOT_EXECUTED, not zero. AO density is a representation in the specified basis/geometry, not an experimental microscopic measurement. State the h/F/S/ERI rules, functional, occupation/density metric, one-electron/Coulomb/exact-exchange/semilocal-XC/nuclear terms and closure residual. Operational conditional-energy differences are not automatically physical bond energies. Judge fixed-point convergence, energy stationarity and stable minima separately. The stored sample has not shown an obvious tangent energy-derivative mismatch, so that is not established as the sole failure cause.

Conditional Compute remains closed; these diagnoses do not prove that no qualified root exists and do not automatically overturn original P01–P14. External expert consultation was cancelled by the owner and is not an implementation gate. Ordinary closed-shell singlet input is a model assumption; complete canonical/virtual-orbital qualification is not implemented, and ordinary wavefunction stability was not executed.
